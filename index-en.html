About HabitLight

HabitLight helps families build children’s self-discipline and good habits through tasks, points, and rewards. Use the home screen for daily tasks, the Statistics tab for progress and charts, and the Rewards tab to redeem points.

FAQ

Q: How do I add a task?

A: On the Home screen, tap the "+" button, choose a category, then add the task.

Q: How do I earn points?

A: Points are added automatically when you complete a task, based on that task’s score.

Q: How do I redeem a reward?

A: Open the Rewards tab, select a reward, and tap the redeem (bag) button. Make sure you have enough points.

Q: How do I switch or add a child?

A: Tap the profile icon at the top left to select a child or add a new one.

Q: Will my data be lost?

A: No. The app saves data on your device. You can also turn on iCloud sync in Settings for a cloud backup.
